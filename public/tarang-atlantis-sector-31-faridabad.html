What is the construction-linked payment plan?

As per the payment-plan image shared for Tarang Atlantis: 10% at booking, 10% at licence, 15% at RERA, 10% at start of the 1st-floor slab, 10% at start of the 4th-floor slab, 10% at start of the 8th-floor slab, 10% at start of the top-floor slab, 10% at start of brickwork, 10% at start of floor and plaster work, and 5% at possession. What payment may be due next on a resale apartment?

The exact outstanding amount depends on payments already credited, the construction milestone achieved and the developer's latest demand.
