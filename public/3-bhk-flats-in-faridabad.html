How to use this 3 BHK guide

Faridabad's 3 BHK market is not one uniform segment. A buyer looking below ₹75 lakh will mostly compare affordable-housing homes and older resale societies. At higher budgets, options may include builder floors, established group-housing societies and larger premium residences. A higher asking price alone does not make a flat or society “luxury”.

Affordable housing and value-focused options

In the lower budget bands, buyers may be comparing affordable-housing societies or older resale apartments rather than conventional premium group housing. Examples discussed for this segment include Maulshree Heights, Amolik Park View, Adore Prosperity / Prosperity Homes and Emerald Anmol. Habitat and other older societies may offer lower entry prices, but condition, upkeep and community facilities need careful inspection.

In locations around World Street where roads and access have improved, the price of affordable 3 BHK homes may already be higher than the lowest budget band. Buyers should check the exact society, approach road, parking and total payable amount.

Builder floors and resale societies

BPTP Elite Floors and resale apartments in older SRS communities can be relevant for buyers looking below or around ₹1 crore. Sainik Vihar, Sector 87, is another location to investigate for larger-plot builder floors. A 180 sq yd floor's price can vary substantially by floor level, construction, access, parking, title and independent floor rights.

For any resale property, inspect the actual unit and common areas. Ask for chain of title, sanctioned plan, occupancy/completion documents where applicable, loan/encumbrance status, property-tax and maintenance dues, electricity/water arrangements, parking allocation and transfer/registry process. Take independent legal advice before paying a token amount.

Established group-housing societies to compare

For buyers seeking a larger residential community, options discussed include BPTP Grandeura, Puri Pranayam, RPS Auria, RPS Savana, RPS Palm, Puri Anand Vilas and BPTP Discovery Park. These societies should be compared on their own merits rather than described as identical products.

RPS Savana, for example, may appeal to buyers who value a large community, resident interaction and society events, even though it is an older development. Puri Pranayam, KLJ, BPTP Grandeura and RPS Auria are established group-housing communities; their price and suitability depend on the particular tower and unit.

For 3 BHK homes, sizes can vary significantly — approximately 1,500 sq ft to 2,200 sq ft in many comparisons, with larger layouts also existing. Some larger configurations may be around 3,300 sq ft. Always compare the same area definition and request the floor plan.

Luxury society is not the same as a luxury flat

A high-priced flat is not automatically a luxury flat, and a luxury-positioned society does not mean every resale unit has luxury interiors. Interior finish, furniture, lighting, kitchen, bathrooms and custom fittings may need to be created by the owner separately.

For this guide, “luxury society” refers to a development whose overall lifestyle offering, amenity planning, clubhouse, wellness/recreation facilities, tower experience, services and community environment create a distinct premium proposition. A clubhouse alone or a high price tag is not enough to make that classification.

Based on the market distinction discussed for this guide, BPTP SkyNest is being positioned as a luxury-oriented development to evaluate for its planned lifestyle and amenity offering. Puri Anand Vilas and BPTP Discovery Park may offer a strong clubhouse / community experience, but are not automatically to be labelled the same type of luxury product. Omaxe Forest may offer a resort-style or nature-led premium presence; Omaxe Royal Residency should not be labelled luxury solely by name. All project claims and amenities must be checked against current official project material.

A luxury interior is a separate fit-out decision. Buyers should inspect the actual delivered specification and finished unit, not infer it from a society label.

How to compare the real cost of a 3 BHK

  • Ask for a dated, itemised cost sheet and confirm whether the quote is base price or all-in.
  • Compare carpet area, balcony area and saleable/super area separately.
  • Check floor, view, tower, sunlight, ventilation, parking and furnishing.
  • Include applicable GST (where applicable), stamp duty, registration, maintenance deposits, club charges, parking and other payable charges.
  • Verify RERA registration and current approvals for applicable projects through official records.
  • For resale, check title, outstanding dues, occupancy status, transfer charges and physical condition.
  • Check daily road access and commute; treat proposed infrastructure as uncertain until officially confirmed.

Frequently asked questions about 3 BHK flats in Faridabad

Which 3 BHK options can I explore below ₹70 lakh?

This budget is generally a value-focused search involving affordable-housing projects and older resale societies. Examples discussed on this page include Habitat, Maulshree Heights, Amolik Park View, Adore Prosperity / Prosperity Homes and Emerald Anmol. Are parking facilities guaranteed in affordable societies?

No. Parking arrangements differ by society and unit. Confirm the written parking allocation, actual space and visitor-parking rules before booking.

What can I explore between ₹70 lakh and ₹90 lakh?

Depending on current resale stock, buyers may compare BPTP Elite Floors, selected SRS resale apartments and builder floors in locations such as Sainik Vihar, Sector 87. Verify exact floor, title, parking, construction and condition.

Which established societies should I compare for a 3 BHK?

Options discussed include BPTP Grandeura, Puri Pranayam, RPS Auria, RPS Savana, RPS Palm, Puri Anand Vilas and BPTP Discovery Park. The right choice depends on budget, layout, tower, community facilities and the specific unit.

Does a higher price mean a society is luxury?

No. Price alone does not establish luxury. Consider the overall lifestyle offering, amenity planning, clubhouse, wellness and recreation facilities, services, privacy and quality of the actual development.

Are property-portal prices the latest market prices?

Not necessarily. Portal listings may be old, duplicated or reflect seller expectations rather than completed transactions. Treat them as leads for verification, not as confirmed current deal prices.
