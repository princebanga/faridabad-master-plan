How to compare the real cost of a 3 BHK

  • RERA registration and current project approvals.
  • Exact carpet/saleable area, balcony treatment and floor plan.
  • Current construction/launch status and expected possession timeline.
  • All-in cost including applicable GST, stamp duty, registration, parking, PLC/floor-rise, maintenance deposits and other charges.
  • Road connectivity, schools, hospitals and daily-use infrastructure.
  • Builder payment plan, maintenance terms and resale considerations.

Due diligence before booking

  1. Search the project and promoter on Haryana RERA and compare the official registration details with the property being offered.
  2. Request the exact unit plan, carpet area, balcony area, sanctioned plans and written itemised cost sheet.
  3. Confirm the contractual possession date, payment milestones, cancellation terms and remedies for delay.
  4. Verify title/land rights and any litigation or encumbrance questions with an independent property lawyer.
  5. Check the commute at peak hours. Treat proposed infrastructure as uncertain unless its status and timeline are supported by official information.

These projects are not direct like-for-like alternatives: affordable housing, upcoming projects and premium high-rises can have different eligibility rules, price bands, timelines and obligations.
