How to compare a premium 4 BHK correctly

  • Ask for the exact 4 BHK floor plan and the carpet area stated in the agreement; do not compare only super area.
  • Confirm whether the fourth bedroom is a regular bedroom, staff room, study or a penthouse layout.
  • Compare total cost including base price, PLC, floor rise, parking, GST if applicable, EDC/IDC, club charges, maintenance deposits, stamp duty and registration.
  • Check the number of toilets, balconies, utility area, servant room, storage, lift access and parking allotment.
  • For resale, inspect the actual unit and verify title, seller authority, dues, mortgage release and transfer conditions.
  • Check daily commute, schools, hospitals, shopping and road access using actual routes at relevant travel times.

Frequently asked questions

Which areas should I compare for a 4 BHK in Faridabad?

Start with the exact project and sector rather than assuming all parts of Faridabad are interchangeable. This shortlist includes Sector 80, Sector 31 and Sector 119; compare commute, services, road access and the specific unit.

Are 4 BHK flats in Faridabad available under ₹2 crore?

Availability and final price vary by project, area, floor, configuration and resale seller. We do not publish a fixed under-₹2-crore promise. Request a current, itemised quote for a verified unit.

Is a 4 BHK penthouse the same as a regular 4 BHK apartment?

No. A penthouse may have a different floor plan, terrace or balcony arrangement, pricing and maintenance considerations. Compare the sanctioned plan and agreement area.

Can I find a ready-to-move 4 BHK in Faridabad?

Ready-to-move and resale options are unit-specific. Ask for current inventory and verify occupancy/completion documents, title, dues and physical condition before paying a token.

What should I check before booking a new 4 BHK?

Verify the project on Haryana RERA where applicable, review the sanctioned unit plan, obtain the full cost sheet and payment schedule, and have title and agreement documents reviewed independently.
