OC vs CC: Occupation Certificate vs Completion Certificate

OC • CC • possession • compliance

What is a Completion Certificate (CC)?

A Completion Certificate generally certifies that the real-estate project has been developed according to the sanctioned plan, layout and specifications under the applicable local laws.

What is an Occupation Certificate (OC)?

An Occupation Certificate relates to permission/confirmation for occupation of the building or project, subject to the applicable local authority and rules. The exact document name, scope and conditions can vary by authority.

Before buying a “OC received” property

  1. Ask for the actual certificate.
  2. Verify the issuing authority.
  3. Check the exact tower/phase/plot covered.
  4. Compare with sanctioned plans and RERA records where applicable.
  5. Confirm whether registry/possession requirements are separately satisfied.
