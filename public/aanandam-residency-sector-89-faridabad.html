Buyer questions

Aanandam Residency FAQs

Quick answers based on the supplied brochure and price list. Always reconfirm live inventory and current commercial terms.

Is Aanandam Residency RERA registered?

Yes. The supplied brochure identifies Haryana RERA registration number HRERA-PKL-FBD-764-2025. Check the latest project record directly with Haryana RERA before booking.

Which apartment sizes are listed?

The brochure lists 3 BHK options of 1,520 and 1,890 sq. ft., and 4 BHK options of 1,985, 2,090, 2,150, 2,250 and 2,350 sq. ft. These are brochure-stated saleable areas; verify the exact unit plan and area definition.

Which units have a price in the supplied price list?

The supplied T-10 price list prices only Tower 10 4 BHK + 4 Toilets units of 2,150, 2,250 and 2,350 sq. ft. Prices for other layouts are not stated in that list.

How does the Super Flexi payment plan work?

10% at booking, 10% within 30 days, 20% on start of construction, 30% on completion of super structure, 20% on applying for OC and 10% on offer of possession.
