What the order says

HRERA hearing summary in plain language

A concise explanation of the 05 November 2026 proceeding shown on the official project detail page.

Project presented to the Authority

The promoter’s authorised representative stated that the company was developing an 82.05-acre industrial plotted colony at Village Silani, Sohna, under DTCP Licence No. 166 of 2026 dated 18 August 2026, and referred to layout, zoning and service-plan approvals.

Environmental clearance discussion

The representative stated that environmental clearance was not applicable on the basis presented at the hearing and undertook to submit an affidavit regarding non-applicability. This is a statement recorded in the proceeding; independently verify the final submitted document and applicable approvals.

Conditions before certificate issue

The Authority recorded approval subject to rectification of deficiencies, including corrections in Form A–H, online DPI and other pending items at Serial No. 34. The certificate issuance remained conditional on those submissions.

Industrial plot buyer guide

Documents to verify before booking or purchase

Use this checklist as a starting point. The applicable documents and permissions can vary by plot, use and stage of development.

01 · RERA registration

Check the latest HRERA status and obtain the actual registration certificate, registration number and approved disclosures. Do not treat a hearing-stage approval as an uploaded certificate.

02 · Licence & land

Verify DTCP Licence No. 166 of 2026, licence conditions, land schedule, title/ownership chain, encumbrances and the exact licensed land parcel with qualified professionals.

03 · Approved plans

Obtain the sanctioned layout, zoning plan, demarcation plan and service plans. Match the plot number, dimensions, use and location with the approved drawings.

04 · Industrial use

Confirm permitted industrial categories, restrictions, building parameters, pollution-control requirements and any sector-specific permissions for the intended business activity.

05 · Infrastructure

Check road access, power load, water, sewerage, drainage, fire access and the status of on-site and external development works from official documents.

06 · Cost & agreement

Ask for a complete written cost sheet, taxes and charges, payment schedule, allotment/transfer terms, possession commitments and refund/default clauses.
