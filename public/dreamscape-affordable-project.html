Before you apply or book

Affordable housing buyer checklist

Use this checklist to compare the official project documents with the allotment offer you receive.

Verify the project

Confirm the promoter's legal name, project address, licence number, HRERA registration certificate and validity, approved plans, project land details and any conditions or pending compliance shown on the official record.

Verify the apartment & cost

Match the unit category, carpet area, floor or block, applicable price, instalment schedule, taxes, statutory charges, parking terms and other charges against the current official allotment and cost documents.

Understand allotment terms

Read eligibility conditions, application requirements, draw or allotment procedure, refund provisions, transfer restrictions and the consequences of delayed instalments.

Keep written records

Retain the official brochure, application form, receipts, demand letters, allotment letter and every written clarification received from the promoter or authorised channel.
