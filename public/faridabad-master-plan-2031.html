Faridabad Master Plan 2031: Official Maps, Land Use & Roads

Faridabad’s development plans are planning documents that show proposed land use, road reservations and other planned development for defined planning areas. They are useful for property research, but they do not by themselves prove that a particular plot is approved, serviced, legally transferable or ready for construction.

Important: This is an independent property-research guide, not a government website and not legal or planning advice. Always verify the exact site, khasra, zoning, road alignment and current approvals with the relevant Haryana authority before buying.

How to read a development-plan map

  • Land-use designation: Check the legend and plan notes to understand the designation shown for the exact parcel. Do not infer land use from a nearby sector or coloured area alone.
  • Road reservations: A road shown in a plan may be planned or reserved; that does not prove that it has been built, opened to traffic or acquired.
  • Planning boundary: First confirm whether the property falls inside or outside Municipal Corporation limits and which planning document applies.
  • Amendments: Search for later notifications, revised drawings, court orders and authority records before treating an older plan as current.

Property buyer checklist

  1. Obtain the property's exact location and khasra/parcel details from the seller.
  2. Match the site to the correct official development-plan drawing and legend.
  3. Ask the competent authority to confirm land use, zoning, access and any road reservation affecting the parcel.
  4. Verify title, mutation/revenue records, licences, sanctioned layout/building plans, and applicable RERA registration separately.
  5. Get professional legal and planning advice before paying a booking amount or signing an agreement.

Development plan is not a project approval

A location appearing within a planned sector or near a proposed road does not mean an individual project has received every required approval. Verify the promoter, licence, sanctioned plans, registration status, completion/occupancy documents and current site conditions independently.
