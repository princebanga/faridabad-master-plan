Never judge a property from one document

Property due diligence is a chain: title + approvals + project records + contract + physical property + registration/possession status. One clean-looking document does not replace the rest.

Haryana RERA rules list legal title, allotment/agreement/conveyance documents, sanctioned plans and relevant certificates among project disclosures.

Important: This page is for property education and research, not legal, tax or financial advice. Rules, approvals, lease terms and project documents can change. Always verify the current documents with the relevant authority and, where appropriate, an independent property lawyer.
