What buyers should verify before booking

  • Download and read the current RERA registration certificate and any special conditions or orders.
  • Confirm the promoter's legal entity, land title, licensed land area and approved layout plan.
  • Match the exact plot number, area, dimensions, road access and applicable development charges against signed documents.
  • Ask for the latest payment schedule, agreement for sale, possession/development timeline and refund/default clauses.
  • Visit the site and independently verify infrastructure progress, access roads, utilities and the status of required approvals.
  • Get independent legal review before paying a booking amount or signing an agreement.
Important status note: RERA registration or an uploaded certificate is not a guarantee of investment returns, construction quality, title free from defects, or completion by a particular date. This page does not independently confirm current plot inventory, prices, construction progress, possession dates or availability. Obtain these in writing from the promoter and verify them independently.
