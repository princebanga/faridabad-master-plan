What buyers should compare before choosing a 3 BHK

  • RERA registration and current project approvals.
  • Exact carpet/saleable area, balcony treatment and floor plan.
  • Current construction/launch status and expected possession timeline.
  • All-in cost including GST, registration, parking, PLC and other applicable charges.
  • Road connectivity, schools, hospitals and daily-use infrastructure.
  • Builder payment plan, maintenance terms and resale considerations.
