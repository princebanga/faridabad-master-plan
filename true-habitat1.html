About Anantya by True Habitat

Anantya by True Habitat is an affordable housing project located in Sector 110A, Faridabad. The project is designed to provide modern residential living with thoughtfully planned homes, dedicated car parking and community-focused amenities.

The development comprises Stilt + 14 towers and offers multiple residential configurations including 2 BHK, 2 BHK + Store and 3 BHK homes. Buyers can contact us for the latest unit availability, Management Quota (MQ) quote and booking process.
