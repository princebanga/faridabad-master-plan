Adore Legend-II Sector 119: Pros & Cons for Buyers

Use this as a practical location-and-investment checklist, not a guarantee of future appreciation or development timelines.

✅ Pros

  • Near Atal Bihari Vajpayee Government Medical College: the Chhainsa medical college is a key regional landmark; confirm the driving distance from the exact project gate using the map links below.
  • Planned Jewar Airport connectivity: the Faridabad–Jewar Greenfield Expressway is a planned/under-development regional connection; access time from this site will depend on connecting roads and the completed route.
  • Direct 66-ft road frontage (as confirmed by the page owner): the project is stated to be directly on a 66-foot-wide road. Buyers should cross-check the width and frontage against the sanctioned layout/site documents.
  • Faridabad IMT connectivity: the location may suit buyers tracking the industrial/IMT side of Faridabad; verify actual commute by route and time of day.
  • Construction-linked payment schedule: the shared schedule ties payments to construction milestones. Confirm the latest signed schedule and milestone notices with the promoter.
  • Longer-term growth possibility: buyers with a 4–5 year horizon may watch infrastructure and surrounding development, but growth and price appreciation are not assured.

⚠️ Cons / Risks

  • Nearby development may take time: shops, services, social infrastructure and occupied neighbourhoods may develop gradually rather than immediately.
  • 4–5 years is an estimate, not a promise: area development depends on approvals, infrastructure delivery, occupancy and private investment; timelines can be longer.
  • Not the established World Street belt: this is on the Fafunda/Dayalpur–Chhainsa side and should not be presented as being in the already-developed World Street/Sector 79 area.
  • IMT-oriented location: buyers prioritising established city amenities and daily access to central Faridabad should test the commute before deciding.
  • Airport road is not the same as immediate airport access: the Greenfield corridor is under development and the project’s direct connection, travel time and opening date must be checked.
  • Final price and charges need written confirmation: verify the applicable GST, other charges, full payment schedule and final payable amount before paying.
