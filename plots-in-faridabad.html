What to verify before buying a plot

  • RERA registration, licence/approval status and sanctioned layout.
  • Exact plot size, dimensions, road width, facing and corner/park PLC.
  • EDC/IDC and other charges included or excluded from the quoted rate.
  • Registry, possession and development/OC status where applicable.
  • Title, land-use and encumbrance documents through appropriate professional/legal verification.
  • Actual site access and surrounding development rather than relying only on marketing material.
