Research principles

  • Live ETA is time-sensitive: it can change with traffic, route choice and departure time.
  • 2031 is a plan horizon, not a completion guarantee: proposed roads must not be described as open or certain to be delivered.
  • Location pins must be verified: confirm the exact project entrance before publishing distances or route claims.
  • AI needs a trusted knowledge base: no API keys or private credentials belong in public browser JavaScript. A secure backend and budget review are required before connecting a paid AI or routing API.
