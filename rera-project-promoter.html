RERA Project Promoter

Promoter companies, directors, partners and authorised representatives disclosed in Haryana RERA records — organised promoter-wise and district-wise for easier Haryana-wide research.

Haryana-wide promoter directory: This page connects legal RERA promoter entities with their projects across Haryana. Use the district buttons or search to see where the same builder/promoter entity appears. Current project coverage follows the verified records in the Haryana RERA Project Watch database, with additional official HRERA promoter filings added where cross-district relationships were identified.
Important: A brand/group name and a legal promoter entity are not always the same. We keep legal entities separate unless the records support a clear relationship. “Owner” is not inferred. Director, Partner, Managing Director/CEO and Authorised Representative are shown only where the RERA filing discloses those roles. Ownership/shareholding will be added only when expressly disclosed in the official filing.
Source & disclaimer
Information is compiled from public Haryana RERA records for research convenience. It is not an official HRERA publication, legal advice, ownership/title verification, investment advice or a guarantee of current status. RERA records can be amended, corrected or superseded. Always verify the latest official HRERA record, Form A-H, certificate and proceedings before relying on the information. Faridabad Master Plan does not infer ownership merely because a person or company appears in a promoter filing.
