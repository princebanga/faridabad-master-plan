RERA Project Promoter

Promoter companies, directors, partners and authorised representatives disclosed in Haryana RERA records — organised builder-wise for easier research.

What this page shows: This is a promoter-focused companion to the RERA Project Watch. The information below is taken from public HRERA Form REP-I / project records. “Owner” is not used as a generic label: where HRERA identifies a person as a director, partner, managing director/CEO or authorised representative, we show that exact role. Ownership/shareholding is shown only when the RERA filing expressly discloses it.
Initial verified set: Emerald / Emarald, Amolik and Adore-related entities are being added first. This page is intentionally expandable; related legal entities are kept separate so a brand name is not incorrectly treated as one legal promoter company.
Source & disclaimer
Information is compiled from public Haryana RERA records for research convenience. It is not an official HRERA publication, legal advice, ownership/title verification, investment advice or a guarantee of current status. RERA records can be amended, corrected or superseded. Always verify the latest official HRERA record, Form A-H, certificate and proceedings before relying on the information. Faridabad Master Plan does not infer ownership merely because a person or company appears in a promoter filing.
