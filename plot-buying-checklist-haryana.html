Special warning for plotted projects

Do not rely only on a brochure, map pin, WhatsApp PDF or broker statement. Match the plot number, area, layout, approvals and legal documents. For RERA-registered plotted developments, the promoter is required to disclose relevant project information under the applicable law/rules.

Haryana RERA rules require project disclosures including sanctioned layout plans, legal title documents and relevant completion/occupation certificates where applicable.
