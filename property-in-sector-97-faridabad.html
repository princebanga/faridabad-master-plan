Prince Banga's Ground View

Sector 97 is an emerging premium residential location, but it is still a developing market. The location has long-term potential through its surrounding ecosystem and future connectivity, while some roads and surrounding development are still catching up.

My ground view includes World Street, Amrita Hospital, the Manjhawali/FNG future-connectivity story and the current road-development situation. A freight-corridor underpass is also a consideration; the elevated alignment limits its impact in the relevant stretch, but buyers should inspect the exact approach themselves.

What I noticed on the ground

🏗️ Development

Some surrounding stretches remain under development and road infrastructure is not uniformly mature.

🏙️ Premium ecosystem

World Street, nearby healthcare and the mix of plotted and group-housing options support a premium residential narrative.

🛣️ Future connectivity

Manjhawali/FNG connectivity is a future upside to watch, not a guaranteed current travel-time benefit.

💰 Plot-price context

Our ground discussion placed indicative plot values around ₹1.15–1.50 lakh per gaj. This is market context, not a live quotation.

Sector 97 — Quick Verdict

My view: Sector 97 is interesting for buyers who want exposure to an emerging premium Greater Faridabad location and can hold while development matures. The opportunity is real, but so is the infrastructure/execution risk. Compare the exact project, entry price and current road access before choosing between a plot, low-rise floor and high-rise apartment.

What to verify before booking

  • Any marketing claim, amenity, possession date or commercial term against current project documentation.
