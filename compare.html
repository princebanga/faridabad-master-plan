Soha Olive Homes Analysis

Soha Group is a well-known name with successful deliveries like Neal City and Wonder Walk. This is their first High-Rise affordable project. It's ideal for those seeking a peaceful, green environment away from highway noise, while maintaining easy connectivity to Noida via Manjhawali Road.

Advitya Homes 2 Analysis

Advitya is a veteran in the affordable segment. Following the success of Aagman and Phase 1, their new launch offers unmatched connectivity. Being right on Mathura Road with metro access nearby, it remains a top choice for both investors and end-users.

Advisor's Final Verdict

If your priority is direct highway connectivity and a builder with vast affordable experience, go for Advitya Homes 2. If you prefer a modern high-rise lifestyle closer to the Noida link and green surroundings, Soha Olive Homes is a highly-rated choice.
