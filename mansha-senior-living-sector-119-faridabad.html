What Buyers Should Verify

  • Obtain and verify the actual LOI document, issuing authority, conditions and validity.
  • Check the current HRERA registration status and approved project details before accepting or paying booking money.
  • Confirm land title, licence, sanctioned building plans, total floors and the final unit-area definitions in writing.
  • Ask for a complete all-inclusive cost sheet covering PLC, EDC/IDC, taxes, power backup, parking, IFMS and other charges.
  • Confirm possession timeline, construction-linked milestones, cancellation/refund terms and the agreement for sale.
  • Independently check the road route and actual distance/time to the Greenfield Expressway and onward connectivity towards Jewar Airport.
