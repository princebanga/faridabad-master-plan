Why the Project Gets Attention

BPTP describes Skynest as a vertical residential development built around biophilic design, with 20 elevated Sky Nests and extensive landscaped amenity areas. The developer announced the project in April 2026 as a Sector 80, Greater Faridabad launch. BPTP's current project page lists it as a new launch at ₹4.8 Cr* onwards.

For buyers, the key points to verify are the exact unit size, floor, view, PLC/floor-rise, payment plan, all-in cost, possession terms and availability before making any commitment.
